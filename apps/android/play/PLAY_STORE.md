**Full description** (4000):
```
Phosphor OS for Android is the phone app for Phosphor OS, the unofficial console-style firmware for the SuperStation One. It works over your home WiFi with a SuperStation One running Phosphor OS.

HOME & LIBRARY
• Pick up where you left off: the last game, favorites and recently played, with Play on TV.
• Browse every system's games, search them all, and start a game on the TV from your phone.
• Fix box art with a photo, edit a game's details, and see what still needs box art.

BOX ART
• Look up box art on the phone, much faster than on the console, and send it to the SuperStation.
• Choose the sources and their order; it keeps going in the background with a notification.

SAVES
• Back up the PlayStation memory card in port 1 or 2 and put one back.
• The SuperStation's save backups: back up now, restore saves or everything.

VIDEO
• MiSTer's video settings explained in plain language.
• Try a setting safely: if the picture is wrong or gone, the old one comes back by itself unless you keep it.
• The SuperStation's video profiles, and which switches on the side of the console a setting needs.

POWER, UPDATES & STORAGE
• Restart, shut down safely, install a Phosphor update or roll back, and restore points.
• Backups of the console's settings files, kept on your phone, with a warning when an update reset one.
• The SD card, NVMe and USB drives: free space, checks and repair.

NOTIFICATIONS
• A power supply problem, a drive nearly full, a Phosphor update waiting, box art done.

PRIVACY
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no account. The app talks to your SuperStation over your own network, and only goes to the internet for the box art sources you turn on.

Requirements: a SuperStation One running Phosphor OS on the same network as your phone or tablet, and Android 8.0 or newer. Pair it with the code shown on the TV (Settings › Remote access).

Phosphor OS is an unofficial, independent project by F3 Digital Systems. It is not made, endorsed or supported by Taki Udon, Retro Remake or MiSTer-devel. SuperStation and MiSTer are the names of their respective owners.
```
